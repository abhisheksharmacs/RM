# Types of Questionnaires

A **questionnaire** is a research instrument consisting of a set of questions used to collect information from respondents. Depending on the purpose and nature of data required, questionnaires can be classified into several types.

## 1. Structured Questionnaire

* Contains predetermined questions in a fixed order.
* All respondents answer the same questions.
* Mostly uses closed-ended questions.
* Easy to administer and analyze.

**Example:**
"How many hours do you use social media daily?"

* A. Less than 1 hour
* B. 1–3 hours
* C. 3–5 hours
* D. More than 5 hours

**Use:** Large-scale surveys and quantitative research.

---

## 2. Unstructured Questionnaire

* Contains open-ended questions.
* Respondents can answer freely in their own words.
* Provides detailed and qualitative information.

**Example:**
"What factors influence your choice of a smartphone?"

**Use:** Exploratory and qualitative research.

---

## 3. Open-Ended Questionnaire

* Respondents provide descriptive answers.
* Encourages detailed opinions and experiences.

**Example:**
"What improvements would you suggest for online education?"

**Advantages:**

* Rich information.
* Reveals unexpected insights.

**Disadvantages:**

* Time-consuming analysis.

---

## 4. Closed-Ended Questionnaire

* Questions have predefined response options.
* Responses are easy to code and analyze.

**Example:**
"Do you own a laptop?"

* Yes
* No

**Use:** Statistical and quantitative studies.

---

## 5. Mixed Questionnaire

* Combines both open-ended and closed-ended questions.
* Provides both quantitative and qualitative data.

**Example:**

1. Rate your satisfaction with online learning (1–5).
2. Explain the reason for your rating.

**Use:** Most modern research studies.

---

## 6. Pictorial Questionnaire

* Uses pictures, symbols, or illustrations instead of text.
* Useful for children, illiterate respondents, or cross-cultural studies.

**Example:**
😊 Very Happy | 😐 Neutral | ☹️ Unhappy

**Use:** Educational and psychological research.

---

## 7. Online (Electronic) Questionnaire

* Distributed through email, websites, Google Forms, or survey platforms.
* Responses are collected electronically.

**Advantages:**

* Low cost.
* Quick data collection.
* Automatic data storage.

**Example:** Google Forms survey.

---

## 8. Paper-Based Questionnaire

* Printed and physically distributed.
* Respondents fill it manually.

**Advantages:**

* Suitable where internet access is limited.
* Easy to administer in classrooms.

---

## Conclusion

Questionnaires are important tools for data collection in research. The choice of questionnaire depends on the research objectives, target respondents, available resources, and the type of data required. Structured and closed-ended questionnaires are preferred for quantitative studies, while unstructured and open-ended questionnaires are suitable for qualitative investigations.
