# Scientific Conduct (वैज्ञानिक आचरण)

## English Definition

**Scientific Conduct** refers to the ethical, honest, responsible, and professional behavior that researchers must follow while conducting, documenting, analyzing, reporting, and publishing research. It ensures that scientific work is carried out with integrity, transparency, accuracy, and respect for established ethical standards.

Scientific conduct requires researchers to:

* Collect and report data honestly.
* Avoid fabrication, falsification, and plagiarism.
* Maintain accurate research records.
* Respect participants, animals, and the environment.
* Disclose conflicts of interest.
* Give proper credit to contributors.
* Follow ethical and legal guidelines.
* Report findings truthfully, whether the results support the hypothesis or not.

### Academic Definition

> **Scientific Conduct is the set of ethical principles, professional standards, and responsible practices that guide researchers in planning, conducting, analyzing, documenting, and communicating scientific investigations honestly, objectively, and transparently.**

## हिंदी में परिभाषा

**वैज्ञानिक आचरण (Scientific Conduct)** से तात्पर्य उन नैतिक, ईमानदार, जिम्मेदार और पेशेवर व्यवहारों से है जिनका पालन शोधकर्ता (Researcher) शोध कार्य के दौरान करते हैं। इसका उद्देश्य यह सुनिश्चित करना है कि शोध सत्य, निष्पक्ष, विश्वसनीय और समाज के लिए उपयोगी हो।

वैज्ञानिक आचरण में शोधकर्ता को:

* डेटा को ईमानदारी से एकत्रित और प्रस्तुत करना चाहिए।
* झूठे डेटा (Fabrication) का निर्माण नहीं करना चाहिए।
* डेटा में हेरफेर (Falsification) नहीं करनी चाहिए।
* साहित्यिक चोरी (Plagiarism) से बचना चाहिए।
* सभी रिकॉर्ड सही तरीके से बनाए रखने चाहिए।
* प्रतिभागियों एवं पशुओं के अधिकारों का सम्मान करना चाहिए।
* हितों के टकराव (Conflict of Interest) को घोषित करना चाहिए।
* सभी सहयोगियों को उचित श्रेय देना चाहिए।
* शोध परिणामों को सत्य रूप में प्रकाशित करना चाहिए।

### हिंदी की शैक्षणिक परिभाषा

> **वैज्ञानिक आचरण वह नैतिक एवं पेशेवर व्यवहार है जिसके अंतर्गत शोधकर्ता शोध की योजना बनाने, डेटा एकत्र करने, विश्लेषण करने, रिकॉर्ड रखने तथा परिणाम प्रकाशित करने के दौरान ईमानदारी, निष्पक्षता, पारदर्शिता और उत्तरदायित्व का पालन करता है।**

# Scenario (Example)

## Scenario 1: Good Scientific Conduct

A researcher wants to study whether a new teaching method improves student performance.

He conducts an experiment on 100 students and finds that only 60 students show improvement while 40 students show no improvement.

Even though the results are not as impressive as expected, he reports the actual findings honestly in his research paper.

He keeps all records, cites previous studies properly, and acknowledges the contributions of his team members.

### Why is this Scientific Conduct?

Because the researcher:

✅ Reported data honestly
✅ Did not manipulate results
✅ Gave proper citations
✅ Maintained transparency
✅ Followed ethical standards

---

## Scenario 2: Violation of Scientific Conduct

A pharmaceutical researcher tests a new medicine.

The experiment shows that the medicine works for only 50% of patients, but the researcher wants quick approval and funding.

He removes the unfavorable data and publishes only the positive results, claiming a 90% success rate.

### Why is this Unethical?

The researcher has violated scientific conduct by:

❌ Falsifying data
❌ Misleading the scientific community
❌ Hiding important information
❌ Violating research ethics

Such actions can lead to:

* Retraction of the research paper
* Loss of professional reputation
* Funding cancellation
* Legal consequences

# Key Elements of Scientific Conduct

| Principle       | Meaning                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Honesty         | Reporting data truthfully                          |
| Objectivity     | Avoiding personal bias                             |
| Integrity       | Maintaining consistency between actions and values |
| Transparency    | Clearly documenting methods and results            |
| Accountability  | Taking responsibility for research activities      |
| Fairness        | Giving proper credit to contributors               |
| Confidentiality | Protecting sensitive information                   |
| Respect         | Respecting participants, animals, and society      |

---

# Exam-Oriented 5-Mark Answer

**Scientific Conduct** is the ethical and professional behavior expected from researchers while conducting and reporting scientific research. It involves honesty, integrity, objectivity, transparency, accountability, and respect for ethical guidelines. Researchers must report data accurately, avoid fabrication, falsification, and plagiarism, maintain proper records, and provide appropriate credit to contributors. Scientific conduct ensures that research findings are reliable, trustworthy, and beneficial to society.

**Example:** A researcher who publishes all experimental results honestly, including negative findings, demonstrates good scientific conduct.
