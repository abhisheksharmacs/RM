# Steps in Designing a Questionnaire

A **questionnaire** should be carefully designed to ensure that accurate, reliable, and relevant data are collected from respondents.

## 1. Define the Research Objectives

* Clearly identify the purpose of the study.
* Determine what information needs to be collected.
* Ensure every question contributes to the research objectives.

**Example:** If the objective is to study students' satisfaction with online learning, questions should focus on teaching quality, technology, and learning experience.

---

## 2. Identify the Target Respondents

* Determine who will answer the questionnaire.
* Consider respondents' age, education, language, and background.

**Example:** A questionnaire for school students should use simpler language than one designed for university professors.

---

## 3. Determine the Information Required

* List all variables and data needed.
* Decide whether demographic, behavioral, attitudinal, or factual information is required.

**Example:** Age, gender, study hours, satisfaction level, and academic performance.

---

## 4. Choose the Type of Questions

* Select appropriate question formats:
  * Open-ended
  * Closed-ended
  * Multiple-choice
  * Rating scale
  * Dichotomous (Yes/No)

**Example:** "Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5."

---

## 5. Draft the Questions

* Use clear, simple, and unbiased language.
* Avoid ambiguous or leading questions.
* Ask one thing at a time.

**Poor Question:**
"Do you think online classes are convenient and effective?"

**Better Question:**

1. Are online classes convenient?
2. Are online classes effective?

---

## 6. Arrange Questions Logically

* Start with simple and interesting questions.
* Place sensitive or personal questions near the end.
* Group related questions together.

Typical Order:

1. Introduction
2. General questions
3. Specific questions
4. Sensitive questions
5. Demographic information

---

## 7. Design the Questionnaire Layout

* Make the questionnaire visually appealing and easy to follow.
* Provide clear instructions.
* Use proper numbering and spacing.

---

## 8. Pilot Test (Pretest) the Questionnaire

* Conduct a trial run with a small sample.
* Identify confusing or ineffective questions.
* Check completion time and response quality.

---

## 9. Revise and Improve

* Modify questions based on feedback from the pilot test.
* Remove unnecessary questions.
* Correct wording and formatting issues.

---

## 10. Finalize and Administer

* Prepare the final version.
* Distribute through paper forms, interviews, email, or online survey tools.
* Collect and analyze responses.

---

## Flow of Questionnaire Design

**Research Objectives → Target Respondents → Information Required → Question Type Selection → Draft Questions → Logical Arrangement → Layout Design → Pilot Testing → Revision → Final Questionnaire**

## Exam-Oriented Answer (5 Marks)

The main steps in questionnaire design are:

1. Define research objectives.
2. Identify target respondents.
3. Determine required information.
4. Choose appropriate question types.
5. Draft clear and unbiased questions.
6. Arrange questions logically.
7. Design the questionnaire layout.
8. Conduct pilot testing.
9. Revise based on feedback.
10. Finalize and administer the questionnaire.

A well-designed questionnaire improves the accuracy, reliability, and validity of research data.
